The 7-stage funnel and what the data covers

The data pack measures two of the seven stages directly and one more through a proxy. Dashed rows have no records at all.

To finish the funnel, ask Pristyn Care for: an ad and enquiry log, diagnostics orders, surgery bookings and completions, and a follow-up flag. Until then, drop-off at stages 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 cannot be stated, and any number for them would be a guess.

Method and limits

Attended means no_show is 0. This matches consultation minutes above zero on all 2,000 rows, so the data holds no booked-and-seen-but-not-consulted group.

Tests. Chi-square for each factor, a two-proportion z-test for one specialty against the rest, Wilson 95% ranges, and a Bonferroni correction across eight specialties.

Follow-up is a proxy. The data has no follow-up flag, so a return means an attended patient with a later booking. Appointments stop on 12 January 2026 and the median gap between visits is 98 days, so patients seen late in 2025 had little time to come back. Read 15.1% (January to June) as the better estimate, and 10.0% as a floor.

Small groups. Each doctor has 10 to 32 appointments, and each specialty has about 20 appointments a month, so single doctors and single months swing widely. January 2026 holds 43 appointments and is left off the trend.
